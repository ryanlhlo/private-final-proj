About Project Gutenberg

Project Gutenberg is an online library offering tens of thousands of free eBooks, focusing mainly on classic literature and historical texts that are in the public domain.

The project was started by Michael Hart in 1971, who pioneered the idea of digital books. His original vision still shapes Project Gutenberg today.

Since then, volunteers around the world have worked to scan, type, proofread, and format texts so that anyone can read them on computers, phones, and dedicated e-readers.

The entire collection is free to access and free to keep. There are no subscriptions or paywalls.
